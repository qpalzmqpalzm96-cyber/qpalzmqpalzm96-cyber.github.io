Privacy Policy for BrickLike

This privacy policy applies to the BrickLike app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by the Developer (JunSS) as a free service with optional in-app purchases. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use
The Application does not explicitly collect personal information. Game progress and purchased in-game currency are stored on your device and, if you use cloud save, in the cloud (see below). The results of your runs are uploaded to a world ranking (see below). In addition, the Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, such as device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address and usage data.

Cloud Save
To keep your progress when you reinstall the app or change devices, the Application signs in to Unity Gaming Services, using your Google Play Games account if you are signed in to it, or otherwise an anonymous player ID created for the installation. Your game data (in-game currency, purchased items, game progress and your own run records) is saved to Unity Cloud Save under that player ID. This data is not shown to other players. The Application does not receive your email address or real name from Google Play Games. To have your cloud data deleted, contact the Developer at the email below.

World Ranking
When a run ends, and when you open the records screen, the Application uploads your result to a leaderboard in Unity Gaming Services (Unity Leaderboards) under the same player ID, together with a randomly generated player name. The uploaded data is the score (the floor reached, or the blocks destroyed in Endless Mode) and a summary of the run: weapons and gems with their levels, items, the starting weapon, each weapon's share of the damage, the time survived and the app version. Only your best result is kept. Other players can see your rank, score, weapons and gems, and the Developer uses this data to improve game balance. It does not include your email address or real name. To have your ranking data deleted, contact the Developer at the email below.

Third-party services used by the Application:
- Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google AdMob (advertising): https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
- Google Play Billing (in-app purchases): payments are processed by Google Play. The Developer does not receive your payment card details.
- Google Play Games Services (sign-in for cloud save): https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Unity Gaming Services (sign-in, cloud save and world ranking): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Advertising and Consent
The Application shows ads served by Google AdMob. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time. You can also reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

Children
The Application is not directed at children under 13, and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Developer through the contact email listed on the Application's Google Play store page.


BrickLike 개인정보처리방침

이 개인정보처리방침은 개발자(JunSS)가 무료(선택적 인앱 결제 포함)로 제공하는 모바일 앱 BrickLike(이하 "앱")에 적용됩니다.

정보 수집 및 이용
앱은 개인정보를 직접 수집하지 않습니다. 게임 진행 상황과 구매한 게임 재화는 사용자의 기기에 저장되며, 클라우드 저장을 쓰면 클라우드에도 저장됩니다(아래 참고). 판의 결과는 전세계 순위표에 올라갑니다(아래 참고). 또한 앱이 사용하는 아래 제3자 서비스가 광고 ID 등 기기 식별자, IP 주소, 이용 정보를 수집할 수 있습니다.

클라우드 저장
앱을 다시 설치하거나 기기를 바꿔도 진행 상황이 이어지도록, 앱은 Unity Gaming Services에 로그인합니다. Google Play 게임즈에 로그인되어 있으면 그 계정으로, 아니면 설치마다 만들어지는 익명 플레이어 ID로 로그인합니다. 게임 데이터(게임 재화, 구매한 상품, 게임 진행 상황, 내 기록)는 그 플레이어 ID로 Unity Cloud Save에 저장되며 다른 플레이어에게 보이지 않습니다. 앱은 Google Play 게임즈에서 이메일 주소나 실명을 받지 않습니다. 클라우드 데이터 삭제를 원하면 아래 이메일로 연락해 주세요.

전세계 순위
판이 끝날 때와 내 기록 화면을 열 때, 앱은 같은 플레이어 ID로 Unity Gaming Services 순위표(Unity Leaderboards)에 결과를 올리며, 순위표에는 무작위로 만들어진 플레이어 이름이 함께 붙습니다. 올라가는 정보는 점수(진 층, 무한 모드는 부순 블럭 수)와 판 구성 요약(무기·보석과 레벨, 아이템, 시작 무기, 무기별 데미지 비율, 버틴 시간, 앱 버전)입니다. 사람마다 가장 좋은 기록 하나만 남습니다. 다른 플레이어에게는 순위·점수와 무기·보석이 보일 수 있으며, 개발자는 이 정보를 게임 밸런스 개선에 씁니다. 이메일 주소나 실명은 포함되지 않습니다. 순위표 기록 삭제를 원하면 아래 이메일로 연락해 주세요.
